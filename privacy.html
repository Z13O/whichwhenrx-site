Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

The short version

whichwhenRx keeps your health information on your phone, encrypted. We have no account system and no server that stores your information, we do not sell or share your data, and we do not use advertising or tracking.

What the App stores, and where

People you add (username, optional first and last name, month and year of birth and optional day, daily routine, health factors, allergies, optional caregiver contact), medicines and supplements, doses taken or skipped, reminders, refill counts, notes, and your settings. All of this is stored only on your device, encrypted with a key kept in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

What leaves your device

To look up products, the App sends only barcode numbers, product numbers (NDC) or search words, over an encrypted connection, to: the U.S. FDA (openFDA), the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements (Dietary Supplement Label Database), Open Food Facts, and UPCitemdb. These services receive the request and your device's internet address, as any website does, under their own privacy policies. No names, doses, health factors or other personal details are sent. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy and security → Look up products online.

If you choose to share or save a medication summary or daily plan, or to text a caregiver, the App hands the content to the app or service you pick. What happens next is governed by that service.

If you choose to send feedback, your email app sends us the message you write plus your app version and phone model. Please don't include health details.

Optional tips

If you choose to leave a tip, a secure payment page from our payment provider opens in your web browser. The provider collects your payment details under its own privacy policy; we receive the tip and the provider's receipt details, never your card number, and no health information is involved.

What we do not do

We do not collect your health information on our servers, sell or rent data, use your data for advertising, use analytics or tracking tools, or share data with data brokers.

Notifications and permissions

Camera (to scan barcodes; images are not stored), notifications (reminders, created on your device), Face ID / passcode (handled by your phone; we never receive biometric data), Photos (only to save a summary when you ask). You can change these in your phone's settings.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete any medicine or person at any time. Deleting a medicine removes it and all of its history from your device. Settings → Your data → Delete all my data erases everything the App stores, including the encryption key. Copies you shared outside the App, and earlier device backups, are outside the App's control.

Children

The App is for adults 18 and over. An adult may add a child's medicines to manage them on the adult's own phone. We do not knowingly allow children to use the App.

Security

We use encryption, an optional app lock, and other safeguards, but no method is perfectly secure.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, the App will ask you to review and agree again.
