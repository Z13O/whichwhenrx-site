Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email oluochoscar@yahoo.com, or use Settings → Send feedback in the app. Please don't include personal health details.

Frequently asked

Where is my data stored? Only on your phone, encrypted. We have no servers that hold it.

How do I delete my data? Settings → Your data → Delete all my data. Deleting a single medicine removes all of its history.

Reminders don't appear. Check that notifications are allowed for whichwhenRx in your phone's Settings, and open the app at least every couple of weeks so it can schedule upcoming reminders.

A scanned product is wrong. Edit any detail before saving; the app remembers your correction for that barcode. Every detail shows its source so you can check it.

Is this medical advice? No. See the Medical Disclaimer; always follow your prescriber and your labels.
