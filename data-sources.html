Data Sources

Last updated October 2, 2026

U.S. FDA (openFDA)

Drug label information from openFDA, a service of the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. openFDA data should not be relied on to make decisions about medical care; always speak to a health provider about the risks and benefits of FDA-regulated products. Use of this data does not imply endorsement by the FDA.

NIH Dietary Supplement Label Database

Supplement label information from the Dietary Supplement Label Database, Office of Dietary Supplements, National Institutes of Health. Use of this data does not imply endorsement by the NIH.

Open Food Facts

Some product names and details from Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

UPCitemdb

Some retail product names from UPCitemdb (upcitemdb.com).

DailyMed

Links to full drug labels on DailyMed, U.S. National Library of Medicine. Timing and interaction advice for medicines cites the FDA-approved label for that medicine on DailyMed.

NIH fact sheets

Supplement advice cites fact sheets from the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements and the NIH National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health.
