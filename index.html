Which med or supplement, and when.

Reminders for prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines and supplements, with photos to tell pills apart and safety checks that show their sources.

Free, with every feature included. No account. Everything stays encrypted on your phone.

Download on theApp Store

What it does

  • Gentle reminders with Take, Snooze or Skip, under a sky that matches the time of day
  • Photos of each pill and its label, plus a finder to identify loose pills in a travel case or organizer
  • Safety checks for interactions, doubled-up nutrients and doses too close together, each with its source
  • Scan, search or type it in: barcode, QR code, name search or manual entry
  • Family and caregivers: a separate list for each person, and an Everyone view
  • Refill and expiry countdowns, a daily plan, and a summary to show your doctor or pharmacist

Private by design

There's no account and no server holding your information. Your meds, supplements, health factors and photos are encrypted on your phone and never uploaded. You can delete everything in one tap. Read the Privacy Policy.
